I picked up my first brush at twelve, put it down for about fifteen years, and came back to it during a long winter in 2020. It's been my favourite way to switch off ever since.

Most of these were **painted at the kitchen table**, in the evenings after the laptop's closed. There's something about spending three hours on a single cloak that a day of shipping software never gives me: no deploys, no tickets, just thin paints and a steady hand.

I mostly paint [Warhammer 40k](https://www.warhammer.com/en-GB/warhammer-40000) and [Age of Sigmar](https://www.warhammer.com/en-GB/age-of-sigmar), with the odd bust when I want to practise faces. Nothing here is perfect, and that's rather the point.
